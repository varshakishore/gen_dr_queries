It should also be noted that intentionality and integration are not mutually exclusive concepts; instead, one reinforces the other to the extent that some indicators can equally be relevant for both attributes. Indicators of the intentional aspect can be addressed with a 'yes' or 'no' answers, making the measurement and analysis straightforward.
