The core procedure is simple: extract a directional vector from model activations by contrasting prompts that differ along a semantic axis, then add that vector at inference time to shift model outputs in the desired direction. This simplicity has driven broad adoption across mechanistic interpretability (Elhage et al., 2022;Nanda et al., 2023) and alignment (Zou et al., 2023). Underlying this practice is a largely unexamined assumption: that the extracted steering direction is meaningful in a precise sense.
