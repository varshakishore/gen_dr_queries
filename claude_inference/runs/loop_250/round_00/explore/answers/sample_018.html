The development of the systems approach in the social sciences is closely associated with the theoretical contributions of a number of scholars. A particularly significant contribution in this field is the structuralfunctional theory of the American sociologist Talcott Parsons. According to his concept, society represents a system of interacting subsystems that must fulfill certain functional requirements in order to ensure the stability and sustainability of the social order. Parsons formulated four fundamental functions that every social system must perform: adaptation, goal attainment, integration, and latency (pattern maintenance).
