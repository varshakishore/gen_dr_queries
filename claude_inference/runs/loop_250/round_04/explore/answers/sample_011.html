# What “prospective predictive validity” means here

Prospective validity requires that measurements obtained before or early in a training phase predict later outcomes relevant to personalization—such as training response (performance gains, strength/endurance adaptation), risk (overtraining, illness, injury), or readiness (recovery capacity, workload tolerance). Retrospective explanatory power notes associations between biological or questionnaire states and outcomes after the fact without demonstrating that the measure would have enabled better prescriptions ahead in time. To judge personalization claims, we therefore prioritize longitudinal cohorts, randomized or quasi-experimental load-modulation studies, and multi-cycle validations.
