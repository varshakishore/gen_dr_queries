Summary answer
Explainable AI is both a technical and a socio-organizational challenge. Technical methods can make models more interpretable, but core issues—bias, robustness, and trust—arise from data, incentives, deployment context, and human–AI workflows; they are not solved by models alone. Openness about training processes helps: standardized documentation, audits, and governance reduce risks and improve accountability, but disclosures have limits (provider integrity, practitioner vs end‑user comprehension) and can create new hazards (over‑trust, disclosure-driven harms). Making companies less secretive would materially advance XAI, but it is not a silver bullet; a broader stack—data governance, evaluation standards, independent audits, and human‑in‑the‑loop processes—is required.
