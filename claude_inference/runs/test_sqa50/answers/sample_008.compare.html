## 📐 Suggested Research Methodology

To actually *prove* your thesis empirically (for a full dissertation), consider:

- **Quantitative comparison**: Use cohort analysis to compare CLV, churn rate, purchase frequency, and margin contribution between customers acquired via viral/shared content vs. paid ads.
- **Mediation analysis**: Model emotional engagement and trust as mediators between viral acquisition and CLV.
- **CLNV modeling**: Apply the Customer Lifetime Network Value framework to account for second-order referral effects.
- **Industry cases**: Dropbox, Airbnb, and PayPal serve as well-documented comparative case studies.
