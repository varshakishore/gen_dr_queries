What determines economic viability
Economic viability must compare incremental revenues and avoided costs of valorization against a credible alternative, typically landfilling under prevailing local fees and logistics. Valorization projects that stabilize, separate, or transform drill cuttings can reduce or avoid disposal charges and create product value, but only if the treated product meets quality and regulatory targets and the total cost (capital, energy, and operations) is justified by market or regulatory revenues.
