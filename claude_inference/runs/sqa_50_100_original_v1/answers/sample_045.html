- Reducing hallucinations often begins with the source data. # Training-Time Signals for Faithfulness

- Reinforcement learning from human feedback (RLHF) has been used to align summarization outputs with user preferences for faithfulness, often by rewarding concise, faithful coverage and penalizing fabrications. Empirical reports in abstractive summarization show that RLHF-style preference optimization can reduce hallucinations relative to baseline finetuning by encouraging adherence to the source document and improving factuality signals <Model name="Anthropic" version="claude-3-7-sonnet-20250219">.

- Directly shaping the objective to promote faithfulness helps. # Domain Adaptation and Document-Level Consistency

- Using domain-adapted data improves grounding. Fine-tuning on domain-specific corpora (e.g., biomedical, finance) provides stronger in-domain signals that correlate with factual knowledge, which in turn reduces hallucinations compared to generic pretraining alone <Model name="Anthropic" version="claude-3-7-sonnet-20250219">.

- Document-level training signals further curb hallucinations by constraining the summary to be consistent with the entire document. Distilling from document-level models or using cross-document consistency losses reduces intra-summary contradictions and spurious edits, yielding more faithful outputs than sentence-level training alone <Model name="Anthropic" version="claude-3-7-sonnet-20250219">.

# Self-Training, Distillation, and Retrieval-Augmented Learning

- Self-training on predicted summaries and distillation from stronger teachers can improve faithfulness. Iteratively training on high-confidence predictions and aligning to a teacher model's behavior reduces hallucinations by propagating more consistent and accurate generation patterns through data amplification <Model name="Anthropic" version="claude-3-7-sonnet-20250219">.

- Retrieval-augmented training that aligns model outputs with high-quality reference segments or external knowledge sources reduces unsupported claims in abstractive summaries by constraining generation to supported content during training <Model name="Anthropic" version="claude-3-7-sonnet-20250219">.

# Practical Recipe and Trade-offs

- A practical approach is to combine curated, high-quality domain data with document-level consistency signals; add RLHF-style preference optimization or faithfulness-specific penalties to reduce fabrications; and apply self-training/distillation to stabilize and propagate faithful behavior. This combination has been observed to reduce hallucinations while maintaining or improving compression and fluency in abstractive summarization <Model name="Anthropic" version="claude-3-7-sonnet-20250219">.

- Trade-offs include the cost and scalability of preference learning and manual filtering, the risk that overemphasis on faithfulness leads to under-summary or rigid paraphrasing, and the challenge of reliably evaluating factuality and hallucinations at scale. Document-level and multi-objective training can mitigate some issues but may increase training compute and data annotation requirements <Model name="Anthropic" version="claude-3-7-sonnet-20250219">.

- Improving training data (quality filtering, domain adaptation, document-level consistency), adding faithfulness-oriented training signals (penalties, RLHF), and leveraging self-training/distillation and retrieval alignment are all effective levers to reduce hallucinations in abstractive summarization.
