Identification of personality traits in the lexical approach is guided by two criteria: synonym frequency (i.e., the more important is a personality attribute, the more synonyms are used to describe it within the language) and cross-cultural universality (i.e., the most phenotypic attributes are typically codified in terms in the languages of different cultures).
