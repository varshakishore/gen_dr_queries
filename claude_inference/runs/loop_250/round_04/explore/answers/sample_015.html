Intuition and categorical perspective
- The equality of homotopy fibers can be seen through the “adjunction” between loops and mapping spaces: forming based loops is left adjoint to mapping into a space, so homotopy fibers over loops convert into loop spaces of homotopy fibers. This is mirrored by the fact that homotopy fibers commute with homotopy pullbacks and with looping, so the construction “hofiber over β” of LX→LY is equivalent to first looping the homotopy fiber of the induced map on fibers, yielding ΩF. Summary answer
- Yes—under the standard hypotheses (Y path-connected with the homotopy type of a CW complex, f a (homotopy) fibration with homotopy fiber F), the homotopy fiber of LX→LY over any non-constant basepoint is homotopy equivalent to the based loop space of the homotopy fiber of X→Y, up to a contractible space of choices determined by homotopies from the loop to a basepoint. In other words, there is a homotopy equivalence hofib(LX→LY over β) ≃ ΩF, unique once you fix a trivialization of β (contractible set of choices). If the basepoint is constant, the identification refines further to ΩF with a contractible twisting by based homotopy data.
