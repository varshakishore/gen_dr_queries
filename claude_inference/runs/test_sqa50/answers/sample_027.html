Bottom line
No single, universally accepted self-report questionnaire measures memory, organized thinking, creativity, and intuition as fully distinct constructs with unified reliability. Instead, use a validated, multi-instrument battery that integrates: (a) creativity inventories (Randsip’s Creativity Questionnaire; Torrance elaboration/organization), (b) dual-process intuition measures (REI experientiality plus MBTI Intuition/TF), (c) organized thinking/executive structure (WMQ executive/attention and Torrance elaboration), and (d) memory (WMQ storage/attention and SAM episodic/semantic). This battery is grounded in instruments with established reliability and convergent validity, and can be administered and scored to yield distinct, interpretable composite constructs.
