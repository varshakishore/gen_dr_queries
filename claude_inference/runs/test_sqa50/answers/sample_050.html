Autism diagnostic instruments developed and validated mainly in children do not transfer consistently to adults because symptom expression, cognitive profiles, and diagnostic frameworks change across development, and measurement tools are tuned to child-specific behaviors and language levels. These factors shift sensitivity and specificity and often require confirmatory, adult-appropriate instruments and clinical integration to maintain accuracy in adults.
