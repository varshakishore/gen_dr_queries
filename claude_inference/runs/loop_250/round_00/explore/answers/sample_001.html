# Why Representativeness Matters and What It Is Not

Representativeness is central to generalization: if a dataset fails to cover the diversity and distribution of the target population, conclusions and model performance on that benchmark can be misleading, and evaluation can become biased toward models that exploit dataset-specific artifacts rather than underlying phenomena.
