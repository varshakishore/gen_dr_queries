## 📌 ADMINISTRATION INSTRUCTIONS

**Purpose:** To identify areas where teachers need professional development, training, or additional support across key domains of contemporary teaching practice.

**Respondents:** Practicing classroom teachers at any level (primary, secondary, post-secondary).

**Instructions to Respondents:**
> *"The following statements describe specific professional tasks and competencies associated with contemporary teaching. For each statement, please indicate whether you feel you have a professional development need in that area. There are no right or wrong answers. Your responses will be used solely to plan relevant and targeted training. Please respond to ALL items."*
>
> ✅ **YES** = I identify this as a training/professional development need for me.
> ❌ **NO** = I do not currently identify this as a training/professional development need for me.

**Estimated completion time:** 8–12 minutes
**Recommended sample size for institutional use:** ≥ 30 teachers (for meaningful aggregate profiling)
