# Overview and Definitions

Nano-refrigerants are conventional refrigerants or refrigerant–oil mixtures with dispersed nanometer-scale solid or liquid nanoparticles intended to enhance thermophysical properties and heat transfer without changing the refrigeration cycle topology. Among oxide additives, copper oxide (CuO) and alumina (Al2O3) nanoparticles are widely studied; CuO typically yields strong heat-transfer enhancements in boiling and condensation but can elevate viscosity and pressure-related parameters, while Al2O3 often delivers more modest gains with better oil compatibility at comparable mass/volume fractions.
