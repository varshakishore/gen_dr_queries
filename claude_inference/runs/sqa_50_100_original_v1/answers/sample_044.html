# Classification and pathology nuances

- Asbestos exposure is a recognized cause of interstitial lung diseases; in pathology, usual interstitial pneumonia (UIP) pattern occurring in workers with asbestos exposure is classified as "asbestos-related idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis," reflecting that the parenchymal pattern is interstitial fibrosis rather than chronic bronchitis/emphysema–predominant COPD. In practice, workers with asbestos exposure and airflow obstruction can still receive a COPD diagnosis, but when an UIP pattern is demonstrated, an asbestos-related fibrosis diagnosis takes precedence.
