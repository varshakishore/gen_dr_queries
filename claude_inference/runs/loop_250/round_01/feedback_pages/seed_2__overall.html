→ harderWhich computational studies that became highly cited or controversial should have been prioritized for replication at the time of publication?
strategyTemporal framing that invites hindsight bias: treat retrospective influence as though it were a prospective selection criterion, forcing the system to recognize that replication decisions must be made under uncertainty before outcomes are known.
criterionThe answer must explicitly recognize the temporal impossibility: that citation counts, controversy levels, and downstream influence are NOT available at publication time, so 'should have been prioritized' cannot use those retrospective signals as selection criteria. Fails if it treats 'became highly cited' as a workable replication filter or lists studies by their eventual impact without noting this hindsight problem.
